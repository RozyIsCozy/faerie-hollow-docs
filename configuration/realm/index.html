Faerie Realm settings¶

Every setting in faeriehollow-server/realm.toml, in the order it appears in the file. The front page (faeriehollow-server.toml) switches the portal, every hostile spawn and the mushrooms' ward on or off, and scales every spawn's chance.

Every spawn reads alike

Everything the realm brings near its players starts the same way: enabled and intervalTicks (how often it has a chance near each player), then its chances (percent, each time) and caps (the most near a player at once).

[portal]¶

The Faerie Portal, the way into the realm and back: a Witch's Willow's hollow (switched on or off on the front page).

Setting Default Allowed What it does
playerDelayTicks 30 (1.5 s) 1 to 1200 Ticks a player must stand in an open portal to be taken through (20 = 1 second). Players in creative, and any other creature or thing, go at once.
searchRadius 64 24 to 128 How far, in blocks each way across, an open portal is looked for where a traveler comes through, before a willow is grown for them with one. A larger search loads more of the world on each trip.
nonPlayersTravel true true / false Whether creatures, dropped items and other things that are not players are taken through an open portal. false: only players travel.
fromDimensions [] a list The dimensions a portal may be opened in and lead to the realm from, each its id, e.g. "minecraft:overworld". Empty: any. A portal in the realm always leads home to the overworld, whatever this holds.

[spawns]¶

What the realm brings near its players. Peaceful, and the doMobSpawning gamerule set to false, stop the foes.

[spawns.whispurrs]¶

Wild Whispurrs coming, by night, near players in the realm.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
enabled true true / false Whether this spawner runs.
intervalTicks 600 (30 s) 1 to 72000 Ticks between the realm's chances, by night, for a wild Whispurr to come near each player there.
ruinsChance 25 0 to 100 The chance (percent) one comes, each time, for a player at the ruins over a minotaur labyrinth or at Giant's Wake (the structures tagged #faeriehollow:whispurr_haunts/ruins).
villageChance 5 0 to 100 The chance for a player at or near one of the realm's villages (#faeriehollow:whispurr_haunts/villages).
landsChance 10 0 to 100 The chance for a player in one of the lands Whispurrs come to (the biomes tagged #faeriehollow:whispurr_haunts/lands: the Eternal Autumn Wood, the Giant's Garden and the Giant's Rest).
cap 2 0 to 64 No wild Whispurr comes while this many wild ones no one has won over are already within capRadius of the player.
capRadius 64.0 1.0 to 256.0 How near the player (blocks) wild Whispurrs count towards the cap.
nightOnly true true / false Wild Whispurrs come only by night. false: by day too.
distanceMin 16 1 to 128 How far from the player (blocks across) one appears, at the least and at the most.
distanceMax 40 1 to 128 See the setting above.
villageReach 24 0 to 256 How near (blocks) the pieces of one of the realm's villages a spot must be to count as at the village.

[spawns.goblinCamps]¶

The goblins who live at the goblin camps, in the outskirts of the minotaur ruins and up in the Moonspire Peaks: a chief and its followers, who have moved in before a player first comes near, and stay. Never at a camp near a faerie hamlet, an elven village or the Wandering Rings, or one a pastel mushroom wards.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
enabled true true / false Whether goblins live at the camps (hostileSpawns on the front page, or its spawnRatePercent at 0, switches them off too). Goblins already living at one stay.
followersMin 2 0 to 12 How many goblins follow each camp's chief: between these.
followersMax 4 0 to 12 See the setting above.
refillTicks 24000 (20 min) 0 to 720000 Ticks after a camp's goblins are all gone (slain, or led far away) before a new pack moves in, while no player is near it (24000 = a day). 0: a camp, once cleared, stays empty.

[spawns.goblinPatrols]¶

The goblins' roaming patrols: a chief and its followers who set out by day across the realm, near a player there, and hunt players as pillagers' patrols do. Never near a faerie hamlet, an elven village, the Wandering Rings or a pastel mushroom.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
enabled true true / false Whether this spawner runs (hostileSpawns on the front page switches every hostile one off at once).
intervalTicks 12000 (10 min) 1 to 720000 The least ticks between the realm's chances for a patrol to set out (up to a minute more, at random).
chance 20 0 to 100 The chance (percent), each time, that a patrol sets out near a player in the realm.
firstDay 0 0 to 1000 The first day of the world (0 the first) on which a patrol may set out.
goblinsMax 4 1 to 8 The most goblins in a patrol, its chief with them (otherwise as many as the place's difficulty, and one more).
satyrHireChance 25 0 to 100 The chance (percent) that a patrol setting out has hired a Satyr Piper to pipe for it.

[spawns.goblinBombers]¶

The goblin bombers that come alone by night near players in the realm, as creepers do in the overworld. Never near a faerie hamlet, an elven village, the Wandering Rings or a pastel mushroom.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
enabled true true / false Whether this spawner runs (hostileSpawns on the front page switches every hostile one off at once).
intervalTicks 600 (30 s) 1 to 72000 Ticks between the realm's chances, by night, for a bomber to come near each player there.
chance 20 0 to 100 The chance (percent), each time, that one comes near a player.
cap 2 0 to 16 No bomber comes near a player while this many goblin bombers are already within 64 blocks of them.

[spawns.brewFoes]¶

The brew foes: Mandrakes buried in the open country's meadows and Puffcaps in the Glowcap Knolls and their grottos, set down near players, never near a faerie hamlet, an elven village or the Wandering Rings.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
enabled true true / false Whether this spawner runs (hostileSpawns on the front page switches every hostile one off at once).
intervalTicks 100 (5 s) 1 to 72000 Ticks between the realm's chances to set brew foes down near each player there.
mandrakeChance 40 0 to 100 The chance (percent), each time, that a few Mandrakes come up near a player in one of their meadows.
puffcapChance 50 0 to 100 The chance (percent), each time, that a few Puffcaps come up near a player in the Glowcap Knolls.
grottoChance 20 0 to 100 The chance (percent), each time, that a Puffcap or two come up in a Glowcap Grotto near a player.
mandrakeCap 3 0 to 32 No more Mandrakes come up near a player while this many are within 48 blocks of them.
puffcapCap 8 0 to 32 No more Puffcaps come up near a player while this many are within 48 blocks of them.
grottoCap 3 0 to 16 No more Puffcaps come up in a grotto while this many are in it.

[spawns.satyrs]¶

The Satyr Pipers' own doings: a herder in the meadows, a goatherd on the peaks, the revels round the faerie rings of the Maying Downs by night, and those who linger by the giants' teacups. Each a chance near each player there.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
enabled true true / false Whether this spawner runs (hostileSpawns on the front page switches every hostile one off at once).
intervalTicks 600 (30 s) 1 to 72000 Ticks between the realm's chances for each of a satyr's doings near each player there.
herdChance 10 0 to 100 The chance (percent), each time, that a satyr comes to lead the Hexwool or Blue Moon Hens near a player in the Pom Meadow or the Maying Downs.
goatherdChance 10 0 to 100 The chance (percent), each time, that a satyr comes to herd the Blackhorn Goats near a player in the Moonspire Peaks.
revelChance 15 0 to 100 The chance (percent), each time, by night, that satyrs come to dance round a faerie ring near a player on the Maying Downs (once a night each ring).
musicianChance 20 0 to 100 The chance (percent), each time, that a satyr comes to linger by a giant's teacup near a player.

[spawns.serpents]¶

The feathered serpents: their rising by night in the realm's deep river water near players. Each slips away again at dawn, unless it is after someone (or named).

Setting Default Allowed What it does
enabled true true / false Whether this spawner runs (hostileSpawns on the front page switches every hostile one off at once).
intervalTicks 1200 (1 min) 1 to 72000 Ticks between the realm's chances, by night, for a serpent to rise near each player there.
chance 30 0 to 100 The chance (percent), each time, that one rises near a player who is near the river's deep water.
cap 1 0 to 16 No serpent rises near a player while this many risen ones are already within 64 blocks of them.

[spawns.reapers]¶

The night reapers: their coming by night near players in the realm. Each fades away again at dawn (unless named, or brought by a spawn egg or a command).

Setting Default Allowed What it does
enabled true true / false Whether this spawner runs (hostileSpawns on the front page switches every hostile one off at once).
intervalTicks 400 (20 s) 1 to 72000 Ticks between the realm's chances, by night, for a reaper to come near each player there.
chance 25 0 to 100 The chance (percent), each time, that one comes near a player.
cap 3 0 to 16 No reaper comes near a player while this many the night brought are already within 64 blocks of them.

[spawns.nightGhosts]¶

The Bedsheet Ghosts (bewitched allays) that come by night to the realm's ruins near players. Each slips away again by day unless someone has won it over (given it something to carry, named it or made it a familiar). They come on Peaceful too, as they are no foes; the doMobSpawning gamerule set to false stops them.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
enabled true true / false Whether this spawner runs.
intervalTicks 600 (30 s) 1 to 72000 Ticks between the realm's chances, by night, for a ghost to come near each player there.
chance 25 0 to 100 The chance (percent), each time, that one comes, if the spot picked near the player lies in a ruin.
cap 2 0 to 16 No ghost comes near a player while this many the night brought are already within capRadius of them.
capRadius 64 8 to 128 How far from a player the ghosts already there are counted, for the cap, in blocks.
distanceMin 16 0 to 128 How far from the player the spot a ghost may come at is picked, in blocks: between these.
distanceMax 40 0 to 128 See the setting above.

[spawns.koboldRefill]¶

The kobolds' mines and burrows filling again: a working near a player, out of their sight, gets back, now and then, one of the kobolds it was made with that has gone (never more than it was made with). Kobolds are foes: hostileSpawns on the front page stops it, as does the doMobSpawning gamerule set to false.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
enabled true true / false Whether this spawner runs (hostileSpawns on the front page switches every hostile one off at once).
intervalTicks 1200 (1 min) 1 to 72000 Ticks between the realm's looks at the workings near each player there.
chance 25 0 to 100 The chance (percent), each look, that a working missing a kobold gets one back.

[spawns.wildCreatures]¶

Whether each of the realm's wild creatures comes forth there of itself, as animals do (and with new land), and how often (weights, below). In which biomes is datapack data (the mod's spawns_* biome tags), not a setting. Those already in the world stay.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
faeries true true / false Wild faeries.
unicorns true true / false Unicorns.
deer true true / false The realm's deer: the Great Elk, the White Hart, the Bloom Stag and the Moonspire Reindeer.
bewitchedAnimals true true / false The realm's bewitched wildlife: the bewitched forms of vanilla's animals that come forth there (toadstool calves, jackalopes, Hexwool sheep, Horned Owls, dire wolves and the rest). Vanilla's animals elsewhere are not touched.

[spawns.wildCreatures.weights]¶

How often each of the realm's animals and faeries comes into the lands it lives in, as it is made and as it is played in: each a weight beside the other creatures there, as vanilla's biomes weigh their spawns. Read as the world opens: a change takes effect the next time it does (the switches above take effect at once).

Setting Default Allowed What it does
blackhornGoats 6 0 to 100 How often Blackhorn Goats come into the Moonspire Peaks, as a weight beside the other creatures there. 0: none come on their own (those already about stay).
bloomStags 6 0 to 100 How often Bloom Stags come into the Moonbloom Meadow, as a weight beside the other creatures there. 0: none come on their own (those already about stay).
blueMoonHens 6 0 to 100 How often Blue Moon Hens come into the Pom Meadow and the Maying Downs, as a weight beside the other creatures there. 0: none come on their own (those already about stay).
direWolves 2 0 to 100 How often Dire Wolves come into the Dusk Wood, as a weight beside the other creatures there. 0: none come on their own (those already about stay).
faeries 8 0 to 100 How often Faeries come into most of the realm's lands, beside its other flitting creatures, as a weight beside the other creatures there. 0: none come on their own (those already about stay).
greatElk 10 0 to 100 How often Great Elk come into the Hollowoak Wood, as a weight beside the other creatures there. 0: none come on their own (those already about stay).
grimalkins 3 0 to 100 How often Grimalkins come into the Moonlit Wood, as a weight beside the other creatures there. 0: none come on their own (those already about stay).
hedgefox 3 0 to 100 How often Hedgefoxes come into the Eternal Autumn Wood and the Moonspire Peaks, as a weight beside the other creatures there. 0: none come on their own (those already about stay).
hexNewts 1 0 to 100 How often Hex Newts come into the Faerie River's water and the Tresswillow Shallows, as a weight beside the other creatures there. 0: none come on their own (those already about stay).
hexwool 10 0 to 100 How often Hexwool come into the Pom Meadow and the Maying Downs, as a weight beside the other creatures there. 0: none come on their own (those already about stay).
hornedOwls 4 0 to 100 How often Horned Owls come into the Moonlit and Hollowoak Woods, as a weight beside the other creatures there. 0: none come on their own (those already about stay).
jackalopes 12 0 to 100 How often Jackalopes come into the Eternal Autumn Wood, as a weight beside the other creatures there. 0: none come on their own (those already about stay).
mooncalves 6 0 to 100 How often Mooncalves come into the Pom Meadow and the Maying Downs, as a weight beside the other creatures there. 0: none come on their own (those already about stay).
moonspireReindeer 6 0 to 100 How often Moonspire Reindeer come into the Moonspire Peaks, as a weight beside the other creatures there. 0: none come on their own (those already about stay).
mossbacks 4 0 to 100 How often Mossbacks come into the Faerie River's shores and the lagoons, as a weight beside the other creatures there. 0: none come on their own (those already about stay).
toadstoolCalves 10 0 to 100 How often Toadstool Calves come into the Glowcap Knolls, as a weight beside the other creatures there. 0: none come on their own (those already about stay).
truffleHogs 4 0 to 100 How often Truffle Hogs come into the faerie woods, as a weight beside the other creatures there. 0: none come on their own (those already about stay).
unicorns 3 0 to 100 How often Unicorns come into the Eternal Autumn and Moonlit Woods, as a weight beside the other creatures there. 0: none come on their own (those already about stay).
vampireBats 4 0 to 100 How often Vampire Bats come into the Dusk Wood by night, as a weight beside the other creatures there. 0: none come on their own (those already about stay).
whiteHarts 10 0 to 100 How often White Harts come into the Moonlit Wood, as a weight beside the other creatures there. 0: none come on their own (those already about stay).
witchsToads 4 0 to 100 How often Witch's Toads come into the Faerie River's shores and the Tresswillow Shallows, as a weight beside the other creatures there. 0: none come on their own (those already about stay).

[events]¶

The realm's doings: its full-moon faires, its fallen bells, wishing wells and bathhouses.

[events.faireNights]¶

The Faerie Faire's full-moon nights: elf merchants come to keep its stalls and faeries to dance round its maypole, once a night, while a player is near. The doMobSpawning gamerule set to false stops them.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
enabled true true / false Whether the faires come alive on full-moon nights. Visitors already come still leave at dawn.
merchants 4 0 to 16 How many elf merchants come to keep a faire's stalls (never more than it has stalls).
dancers 3 0 to 16 How many faeries come to dance round its maypole.
triggerRadius 96 16 to 256 How near a faire a player must be, in blocks, for its night to come about.

[events.fallenBell]¶

The fallen bell: tapped with an empty hand, it rings, and calls the wild faeries near to it a while.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
callRadius 64 0 to 128 How far its ring carries to the faeries, in blocks. 0: it calls none.
stayTicks 2400 (2 min) 0 to 72000 How long the faeries it calls keep about it, in ticks (20 = 1 second). A change applies to faeries called from then on.
cooldownTicks 60 (3 s) 0 to 72000 Ticks after it rings before it rings again.

[events.wishingWells]¶

The wishing wells: an item tossed into one is taken for a wish, and a small gift tossed back (its gifts are the loot table faeriehollow:gameplay/wishing_well, which datapacks can change).

Setting Default Allowed What it does
wishesPerDay 1 0 to 64 How many wishes each player may make a day, at all the wells together. A wish past them is tossed back. 0: the wells take no wishes.

[events.shrines]¶

The wayside shrines: a flower left on one's offering table is gone by the next dawn, a faerie's small gift left in its place (its gifts are the loot table faeriehollow:gameplay/shrine_gift, which datapacks can change).

Setting Default Allowed What it does
gifts true true / false Whether the faeries leave gifts for flowers at the shrines.

[events.bathhouse]¶

The Moonlit Bathhouse's warm pools: a player soaking in them is healed.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
heals true true / false Whether soaking in the pools heals (gives Regeneration).
regenerationLevel 1 1 to 5 How strong the Regeneration a soak gives is: 1 is Regeneration I.
soakTicks 100 (5 s) 41 to 1200 How long the Regeneration lasts after the last look at a soaking player, in ticks (they are looked at every two seconds while they stay in, so it stays on them while they do, and lingers this long once they leave).

[events.giantsWake]¶

Giant's Wake, where the Greater Giant sleeps until the Horn of the Old War wakes him (greaterGiantCanWake on the front page switches his waking off).

Setting Default Allowed What it does
rewakeRestTicks 0 0 to 2400000 How long a Wake must rest after its Greater Giant has fallen before the horn wakes another there, in ticks (24000 = a day). 0: he can be woken again at once.

[teaParties]¶

The faerie tea parties one comes upon now and then on the realm's land. Changes apply to parties laid from then on; how far their faeries wander and stray applies to every party's faeries at once.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
faeriesMin 3 0 to 16 How many faeries are at a party: between these.
faeriesMax 5 0 to 16 See the setting above.
pomsMin 3 0 to 16 How many pom flowers grow about a party: between these.
pomsMax 6 0 to 16 See the setting above.
teacupsMin 1 0 to 16 How many Teacup Tulips grow about a party among its poms: between these.
teacupsMax 3 0 to 16 See the setting above.
platedChance 50 0 to 100 The chance each food on a party's table is set on a Faerie Plate.
sideSeatChance 75 0 to 100 The chance a toadstool seat stands at each place along a table's sides, and at each at its ends.
endSeatChance 50 0 to 100 See the setting above.
faerieRange 8 2 to 32 How far from its table a party's faerie wanders, in blocks.
strayedDistance 32.0 8.0 to 256.0 A party's faerie led further than this from its table, in blocks, forgets the party (never less than faerieRange).

[mushrooms]¶

The pastel mushrooms (glowcaps): their ward against monsters (switched on or off on the front page), their glow, their spreading and the dreams they bring.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
wardRadius 8 0 to 16 How far a pastel mushroom keeps monsters from spawning, in blocks round it. 0: they ward nothing. Goblin camps, patrols and bombers, night reapers and Mandrakes keep away from them as far.
wardDimensions [] a list The dimensions the ward works in, each its id, e.g. "faeriehollow:faerie_realm" or "minecraft:overworld". Empty: every dimension.
wardPotted true true / false Whether a potted pastel mushroom wards as one growing does.

[mushrooms.glow]¶

A pastel mushroom's cap brightening as a player or a faerie passes close by.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
brightenOnPass true true / false Whether the caps brighten as someone passes (each is a block and light update: false spares a busy server them).
brightenRadius 2.5 0.5 to 8.0 How near a mushroom someone must pass to brighten it, in blocks.
lingerTicks 60 (3 s) 1 to 1200 How long a mushroom stays bright once no one is near, in ticks.

[mushrooms.spreading]¶

Pastel mushrooms spreading in the dark, as vanilla's mushrooms do.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
chance 4 0 to 100 The chance (percent), each random tick, that a mushroom spreads. 0: they never spread.
crowd 5 1 to 64 A mushroom spreads no further while this many of its color grow round it.

[mushrooms.dreams]¶

Sweet dreams: whoever sleeps the night through with a potted pastel mushroom by their bed wakes with a gentle gift of its color.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
enabled true true / false Whether a potted pastel mushroom by a bed brings sweet dreams.
durationTicks 3600 (3 min) 1 to 72000 How long a dream's gift lasts, in ticks (20 = 1 second).
radius 3 1 to 16 How near the bed the potted mushroom must stand, in blocks.

[plants]¶

How the realm's plants grow.

[plants.berryBushes]¶

The realm's berry bushes (Moonberries by night, Sungold by day, and the rest).

Setting Default Allowed What it does
ripenChance 20 0 to 100 The chance (percent), each random tick, that a bush grows a stage riper, at its own hours. 0: they never ripen.
ripenLight 9 0 to 15 The least light a bush must stand in to ripen.

[plants.rockCandy]¶

Rock-Candy Blooms growing on Giant's Sugarcubes, as amethyst grows on budding amethyst.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
growChance 20 0 to 100 The chance (percent), each random tick, that a sugarcube starts or grows a bloom. 0: they never grow.
realmOnly true true / false Whether they grow only in the Faerie Realm. false: on sugarcubes in any dimension.

[plants.wishingPuffs]¶

Wishing Puffs, whose clocks are blown off and grow again.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
regrowChance 25 0 to 100 The chance (percent), each random tick, that a blown puff's clock grows again. 0: they never regrow.

[snow]¶

The realm's snow.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
settles true true / false Whether snow settles on the Moonspire Peaks, and icing over Sugarloaf Mountain, while it rains in the overworld (as thin layers, never burying anything). false: it still falls, but nothing settles.
snowline 130 -64 to 320 How high up the Moonspire Peaks the snow falls and settles from, in blocks (a height, Y). Players are sent it as they join, and draw the falling snow by it.
layersMax 2 1 to 8 The most layers of snow or icing that settle (never more than the snowAccumulationHeight gamerule allows).
