Creature settings¶

Every setting in faeriehollow-server/creatures.toml, in the order it appears in the file. The front page (faeriehollow-server.toml) holds the percentages applied to every foe and boss on top of each one's own here.

When health changes apply

A foe's health follows these settings as it joins the world (spawned, or loaded with its land), keeping the share of its health it had: foes already about keep theirs until then.

[foes]¶

Each foe: its health and damage as percentages of its own (on top of the front page's), then how it fights.

[foes.goblin]¶

The goblins of the realm's camps and patrols, bombers among them.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
healthPercent 100 1 to 1000 The health of goblins, as a percentage of their own, with the front page's healthPercent on top. The game holds every creature to 1024 health at most, however high the percentages go.
damagePercent 100 0 to 1000 The damage goblins deal, every kind of blow, as a percentage of their own. 0: no harm at all.
homeRange 48.0 8.0 to 256.0 How far from its camp a goblin's home ground reaches (blocks): whoever gets further away has escaped it.
packRange 16.0 0.0 to 64.0 How near its pack must be to join a goblin's fight, and to hear its chief's war cry (blocks).

[foes.goblin.bomber]¶

The goblin bomber, the realm's creeper (whether its blast breaks blocks is on the front page).

Setting Default Allowed What it does
blastPower 3.0 0.0 to 8.0 How big its blast is, as an explosion's power (a creeper's is 3, TNT's 4). 0: it goes up harmlessly.
fuseRange 3.0 1.0 to 16.0 How near it must come to whoever it is after to light its fuse (blocks).
escapeRange 7.0 1.0 to 32.0 How far off they must get while its fuse burns for it to pinch the fuse out (blocks). Keep it above fuseRange.
catShyRange 6.0 0.0 to 32.0 How near a cat or an ocelot it keeps away from (blocks). 0: it fears no cat. Applies to bombers loaded from then on.

[foes.goblin.roles]¶

Each kind of goblin's own health, blow (hand to hand, before the difficulty's scaling) and armor, before the percentages above. A slinger's sling stones and a hexer's bolts are their own and scale with damagePercent.

[foes.goblin.roles.scrapper]¶

The scrapper, with its fork and pot lid.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
health 24.0 1.0 to 1024.0 Its health (2 is a heart).
damage 4.0 0.0 to 100.0 The harm of its blow.
armor 2.0 0.0 to 30.0 Its armor points.

[foes.goblin.roles.slinger]¶

The slinger, who pelts from afar.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
health 24.0 1.0 to 1024.0 Its health (2 is a heart).
damage 2.0 0.0 to 100.0 The harm of its blow.
armor 0.0 0.0 to 30.0 Its armor points.

[foes.goblin.roles.hexer]¶

Setting Default Allowed What it does

damage 3.0 0.0 to 100.0 The harm of its blow.
armor 0.0 0.0 to 30.0 Its armor points.

damage 5.0 0.0 to 100.0 The harm of its blow.
armor 4.0 0.0 to 30.0 Its armor points.

[foes.goblin.roles.bomber]¶

The bomber (its blast is above).

health 20.0 1.0 to 1024.0 Its health (2 is a heart).

[foes.kobold]¶

The kobolds of the realm's mines and burrows: they warn a player off before they attack.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
healthPercent 100 1 to 1000 The health of kobolds, as a percentage of their own, with the front page's healthPercent on top. The game holds every creature to 1024 health at most, however high the percentages go.
damagePercent 100 0 to 1000 The damage kobolds deal, every kind of blow, as a percentage of their own. 0: no harm at all.
warnRange 8.0 1.0 to 32.0 How near a player must come for a kobold to hiss a warning at them (blocks; less as they sneak).
warnTicks 60 (3 s) 0 to 1200 How long a warned player has to back off before it attacks (ticks; 20 is a second).
warnMemoryTicks 1200 (1 min) 0 to 72000 How long it remembers warning someone: coming back near it in that time, they are attacked without a warning (ticks; never shorter than warnTicks).
summonCount 2 0 to 8 How many miners a plume-caller's shriek raises out of the ground at a time. 0: it calls none.
summonCap 4 0 to 16 The most miners a plume-caller has up about it at once (those it called give no experience).

[foes.minotaur]¶

The minotaurs of the ruins and the labyrinth city: civil until offended, then they charge and toss.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
healthPercent 100 1 to 1000 The health of minotaurs, as a percentage of their own, with the front page's healthPercent on top. The game holds every creature to 1024 health at most, however high the percentages go.
damagePercent 100 0 to 1000 The damage minotaurs deal, every kind of blow, as a percentage of their own. 0: no harm at all.
challengeStrangers true true / false Minotaurs challenge strangers (players without the Horned Helm): guards bar their way and set on them, and the others toss one who lingers. false: strangers are let be (an offense is still answered).
tossDamage 3.0 0.0 to 100.0 The harm of a minotaur's toss (never scaled by the difficulty; none to a player on peaceful).
guardTossDamage 6.0 0.0 to 100.0 The harm of a guard's toss.
tossStrength 0.8 0.0 to 4.0 How hard a toss throws whoever it hits away (blocks a tick, less their knockback resistance).
tossLift 0.6 0.0 to 4.0 How high a toss throws them.
herdRange 24.0 0.0 to 128.0 How near the rest of its herd must be to join in when a minotaur is hurt (blocks).
warnMemoryTicks 2400 (2 min) 0 to 72000 How long it remembers whoever it warned or chased off: an offense again in that time is tossed for (ticks).

[foes.minotaur.strangers]¶

How minotaurs treat strangers, while challengeStrangers is on. Times are counted in whole seconds (20 ticks).

Setting Default Allowed What it does
guardChallengeRange 12.0 0.0 to 64.0 How near a guard challenges a stranger in its sight (blocks).
guardFuseTicks 200 (10 s) 20 to 72000 How long a challenged stranger may stay in a guard's sight before it sets on them (ticks).
lingerRange 16.0 0.0 to 64.0 How near any other minotaur sees a stranger lingering (blocks).
lingerTicks 1200 (1 min) 20 to 72000 How long a stranger may linger in its sight before it warns them (ticks).
lingerGraceTicks 400 (20 s) 20 to 72000 How much longer, once warned, before it tosses them, and again as often while they stay (ticks).

[foes.featheredSerpent]¶

The feathered serpents of the realm's deep rivers (how often they rise is in realm.toml).

slipsAwayByDay true true / false One the night brought slips away by day when it is after no one (one brought by a player, or named, stays).
slipAwayOneIn 200 1 to 72000 By day, the chance each tick that one slips away: one in this many (200 is some ten seconds on average).

health 20.0 1.0 to 1024.0 Every reaper's own health, whatever its kind (2 is a heart), before healthPercent.

[bosses]¶

The bosses: the Greater Giant of Giant's Wake.

[bosses.greaterGiant]¶

The Greater Giant (whether he can be woken is on the front page; his wake is in realm.toml).

Setting Default Allowed What it does
healthPercent 100 1 to 1000 The health of the Greater Giant, as a percentage of his own, with the front page's healthPercent and bossHealthPercent on top. The game holds every creature to 1024 health at most, however high the percentages go.

helmDeflectArc 60 0 to 180 Arrows shot at him from within this many degrees of his face glance off his helm. 0: none do.
plantedSwordTicks 1200 (1 min) 20 to 72000 How long his planted sword stands untended before it crumbles (ticks).

[creatures]¶

The realm's gentle creatures, mounts and merchants.

[creatures.faerie]¶

The faeries.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
tameOneIn 3 1 to 1000 The chance each treat a wild faerie is fed tames it: one in this many (1: the first always does).

[creatures.goldenGoose]¶

The golden geese of Cloudcroft: a source of gold.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
layTicksMin 6000 (5 min) 20 to 1728000 How long a golden goose takes to lay again, at the least and at the most (ticks; 6000 is five minutes).
layTicksMax 12000 (10 min) 20 to 1728000 See the setting above.
goldenEggOneIn 10 1 to 10000 The chance what it lays is a Golden Egg: one in this many (else a gold nugget).
hatchOneIn 8 1 to 10000 The chance a thrown Golden Egg hatches a gosling: one in this many.

[creatures.peafowl]¶

The minotaurs' peafowl.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
produceTicks 6000 (5 min) 20 to 1728000 How long a peahen takes to lay an egg, and a peacock to drop a feather: at least this long, and up to twice as long (ticks).
hatchOneIn 8 1 to 10000 The chance a thrown peafowl egg hatches: one in this many.
fourChicksOneIn 32 1 to 10000 The chance an egg that hatches hatches four chicks, not one: one in this many.

[creatures.unicorn]¶

The unicorns, ridden as horses are. Each percentage scales a horse's own random roll, for unicorns that come from then on.

Setting Default Allowed What it does

[creatures.deer]¶

The deer of every kind (great elk, white harts, bloom stags, moonspire reindeer), ridden as horses are. Each percentage scales a horse's own random roll, for deer that come from then on.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
healthPercent 100 10 to 500 Its health.
speedPercent 100 10 to 200 Its speed.
jumpPercent 100 10 to 200 How high it jumps.
stagOneIn 4 1 to 100 The chance each deer of a wild herd after its first (always a stag) is a stag: one in this many (else a doe).

[creatures.merchants]¶

The realm's merchants: the elves and the minotaurs who trade.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
restockTicks 12000 (10 min) 20 to 1728000 How often a merchant restocks its trades (ticks of game time; 12000 is twice a day).
pricePercent 100 1 to 1000 What each trade asks of the first thing it wants, as a percentage of its own price (at least 1, at most a stack). Applies to every merchant at once, the next time a player trades with it.
