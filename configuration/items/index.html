Item settings¶

Every setting in faeriehollow-server/items.toml, in the order it appears in the file. The front page (faeriehollow-server.toml) holds the cauldron's brewing pace and the wedding cake's and Pixie Draught's switches.

Gear and food

Gear's damage, armor, durability and enchantability and food's hunger and saturation follow these settings at once, for every player (the server sends them its settings). A tree's growth time is set as its sapling is planted.

[cauldron]¶

The Bewitched Cauldron and the brews drunk from it.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
enforceBrewHours true true / false Brews of the day (Sun Tea, an Eternal Autumn sapling) brew only by day and brews of the night only by night, as their recipes say; in a world whose sky stands still (the Nether, the End) neither comes. false: every brew brews at any hour, anywhere. (How long each brew takes is its recipe's, scaled by brewTimePercent on the front page.)
hourCheckTicks 20 (1 s) 1 to 1200 How often a cauldron whose brew waits for its hour looks whether the hour has come, in ticks.

[cauldron.pixieDraught]¶

The Pixie Draught, which shrinks whoever drinks it to a faerie's size (pixieDraughtShrinks on the front page switches it off).

Setting Default Allowed What it does
durationTicks 3600 (3 min) 1 to 24000 How long a drinker stays small, in ticks.
sizePercent 35 10 to 100 How tall a pixie stands, as a percentage of their own height (hitbox, eyes and all). On NeoForge 1.21.1 a change reaches those who drink from then on; on Forge 1.20.1, every pixie at once.
graceTicks 40 (2 s) 1 to 1200 A pixie whose draught runs out with no room to stand at their own height stays small this much longer (in ticks), again and again until there is room.

[altar]¶

The Faerie Altar and its rites.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
lightNeeded 1200 1 to 30000 How much light a Faded Shard must drink to wake: a point a tick while a rite's sky holds over the altar (its hour, clear weather, open sky, its flowers), more with faeries of the monarch's court about.
flowersNeeded 4 0 to 40 How many of a rite's flowers (white for Titania's Vigil, golden for Oberon's Muster) must grow round the altar.
moonFullnessPercent 75 0 to 100 How full the moon must be for Titania's Vigil, as a percentage (100 only a full moon; 75 also a night either side of it; 0 any moon).
onlyInRealm true true / false The altar's rites and summonings work only in the Faerie Realm. false: under any sky with a day and a night.
flowerReach 3 1 to 8 How far from the altar's edge, in blocks, a rite's flowers count.

[altar.court]¶

The monarch's court: faeries of the rite's monarch about the altar.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
faeriesMax 4 0 to 32 Each faerie of the court about the altar makes the shard drink half as fast again, up to this many of them.
reach 8.0 2.0 to 32.0 How far from the altar, in blocks, its faeries are counted and bidden to gather round a summoning.
gatheredMax 12 0 to 32 The most faeries bidden to gather round a summoning.

[weddingCake]¶

The Faerie Wedding Cake.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
wingsTicks 1200 (1 min) 20 to 24000 How long a slice's faerie wings (and their slow falling) last, in ticks. A slice eaten while they are out makes it this long from then. (weddingCakeFlight on the front page says whether they fly.)
liftTicks 40 (2 s) 0 to 200 How long a slice floats its eater upward, in ticks (0: not at all).
nutrition 3 0 to 20 How much hunger a slice fills, in half drumsticks.
saturation 0.2 0.0 to 2.0 How long a slice keeps its eater full: its saturation modifier, as vanilla's foods have (bread 0.6).

[gear.giantsSet]¶

The Giant's Set, the Greater Giant's armor: each piece's protection, as netherite's with a touch more.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
helmetArmor 3 0 to 30 The armor points of its head piece.
chestArmor 8 0 to 30 Of its chest pieces (the pauldron, the mantle).
leggingsArmor 6 0 to 30 Of its greaves.
bootsArmor 3 0 to 30 Of its boots.
toughness 4.0 0.0 to 20.0 Each piece's armor toughness.
knockbackResistance 0.2 0.0 to 1.0 Each piece's knockback resistance (1 shrugs off every knock alone).
durabilityMultiplier 37 1 to 200 How long its pieces last, as vanilla's armor reckons it: a helmet lasts 11 times this, a chest piece 16, greaves 15 and boots 13.
enchantability 15 0 to 100 How readily it takes enchantments (iron armor's is 9, gold's 25).
fullSetReach 0.5 0.0 to 5.0 How much further, in blocks, a player wearing the whole set reaches, to strike and to touch blocks.

[trees]¶

Saplings and how the mod's trees grow.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
faerieTreeGrowthTicks 120000 (1.66667 h) 600 to 2400000 How long a faerie sapling (of any of the four faerie trees) takes to grow full grown, in six stages, in ticks of the world's time (sleeping does not hurry it). (24000 ticks is a day.) Each sapling keeps the growth it was planted with: a change reaches saplings planted from then on.
witchWillowGrowthTicks 72000 (1 h) 600 to 2400000 As faerieTreeGrowthTicks, for a Witch's Willow sapling (the portal's tree).
boneMealChance 45 0 to 100 The chance (percent) bone meal grows a faerie sapling or Witch's Willow, or its young tree, a stage on.
