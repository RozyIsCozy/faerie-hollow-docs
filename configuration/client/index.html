Client settings and keys¶

These belong to you alone: they live on your computer and change nothing for anyone else, even on a server.

The options screen¶

Press Mods on the title screen, select Faerie Hollow and press Config. Every option in your settings file is there, grouped as it is in the file, each with a note on what it does when you point at it:

Audio
The realm's music (on or off, how often it plays, the music of places, caves and nights, the seasons' music, the fight music), and how loud creatures' footsteps and faerie wings are. The seasonal tracks come in the separate Faerie Hollow Seasonal Music resource pack, so without it the realm plays its own music all year.
Visuals
How many of the mod's own particles you see (falling leaves, sparkles, faerie glitter...), and the realm's snow.
Speech bubbles and screen
Familiars' speech bubbles (shown or hidden, only your own familiars', their size, how far off and how long they show) and the familiar portraits on satchel icons.
Accessibility
Reduce flashing: faerie wings about to vanish fade out smoothly instead of flickering.
Advanced
Performance options, and the shaderpacks the mod adjusts its glow and snow for (editable in the file).

Familiars' chatter plays on the vanilla Voice/Speech volume slider (Options > Music & Sounds), so you can turn it down without touching anything else.

Hosting the world?

Opened from the pause menu in single player or your own LAN world, this screen also has a World Settings... button, for that world's server settings. See World Settings, in game.

Keybinds¶

Change them in Options > Controls > Key Binds, under Faerie Hollow.

Action Default key What it does
Open Worn Familiar Satchel B Opens the satchel you're wearing, from anywhere.
Pause or Resume Gathering G Stops your familiar picking up items, and starts it again. Works out in the world, in your inventory and in the satchel's screen. The pause follows you, not the bag: it holds for whichever bag you wear, and is kept when you log out or die.

Note

If the server has switched gathering off for everyone, G only tells you so. Your own pause is remembered for when the server switches it back on.

The client settings file¶

config/faeriehollow-client.toml holds the same options as the screen, for editing by hand or shipping in a modpack.

[audio]¶

Music and sounds.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
realmMusic true true / false Play the Faerie Realm's own music there (its biomes', places', caves' and lullabies). false plays the game's own music in the realm instead. The game's Music volume slider sets how loud either is.
musicFrequency NORMAL How often the realm's music plays: the pause between one piece and the next. RARELY doubles it, NORMAL keeps it as made (ten to twenty minutes in a biome, a minute to three in a place), OFTEN halves it, and CONSTANTLY plays each piece straight after the last. Allowed Values: RARELY, NORMAL, OFTEN, CONSTANTLY
placeMusic true true / false Play the music of the realm's places (the minotaur ruins, the elves' villages, the grottoes, the faerie hamlets...) while you are in one. false plays the biome's music there.
caveMusic true true / false Play the realm's cave music deep under its ground. false plays the biome's music there.
lullabies true true / false Play each realm biome's lullaby at night in place of its music. false keeps its own music all night.
openingPieces true true / false Play a place's own piece first on coming into it (the Moonspire Peaks' caves, the Crumb Caverns), before its other music. false plays its other music from the first.
fightMusic true true / false Play the realm's fight music while a foe is hunting you there, and the Greater Giant's own theme in his fight: it fades in over the realm's music and out again a few seconds after the fight. false keeps the realm's other music playing through every fight.
seasonalMusic true true / false Play the realm's Halloween music in the last week of October (25th to 31st) and its Christmas music in the last week and a half of December (21st to 31st), by your computer's calendar, in the stead of its own, when the Faerie Hollow Seasonal Music resource pack is on. false keeps its own music all year.
footstepVolumePercent 100 0 to 200 How loud the footsteps of the mod's creatures and of bewitched creatures are, as a percentage of their own (no sound plays louder than the loudest the game plays). 0 silences them.
wingSoundVolumePercent 100 0 to 100 How loud faerie wings unfurling, darting and folding are, as a percentage of their own. 0 silences them.
wingExpiryChime true true / false A chime as your own faerie wings start to fade, before they wear off. false: no chime.

[visuals]¶

Particles and effects.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
particleDensityPercent 100 0 to 100 How many of the mod's ambient particles are shown, as a percentage: falling leaves and petals, sparkles from blossom, flowers, portals and faeries, plants' seeds, bubbles and spores, faerie wings' dust and the realm's snowflakes. 0 shows none (Ariadne's Thread still shows a thin line of its own). On top of the game's own Particles setting.
realmSnow true true / false Draw the realm's falling snow and icing while the overworld rains. false: none falls (the weather still comes).
fallingLeaves true true / false Leaves and petals falling from the faerie trees, the Witch's Willow and the tresses that hang from them. false: none fall.

[hud]¶

Speech bubbles and what the screen shows.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
speechBubbles true true / false Show familiars' speech bubbles and play the sound they make with each. false hides both for you (the server can turn them off for everyone). The sound is on the game's Voice/Speech volume slider.
onlyMyFamiliarsBubbles false true / false Show only the speech bubbles meant for you: your own familiars', and a creature's that speaks to you (a faerie wishing you goodnight). false shows every creature's nearby.
speechBubbleScalePercent 100 50 to 300 How big speech bubbles are, as a percentage of their own size (a little smaller than a name tag).
speechBubbleDistance 32 4 to 64 How far away, in blocks, a speech bubble is still drawn.
speechBubbleReadingPercent 100 50 to 400 How long a speech bubble stays up, as a percentage of its own time (longer for longer lines). Raise it to have more time to read them.
satchelPortraits true true / false Draw the bonded familiar's head in the corner of a Familiar Satchel's icon. false: the plain bag.

[accessibility]¶

Accessibility.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
reduceFlashing false true / false Faerie wings about to wear off fade smoothly instead of flickering faster and faster (a flicker of up to about five times a second).

[advanced]¶

Fine tuning: performance and shaderpacks.

[advanced.performance]¶

What your computer spends on the mod's renderers.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
vaseRenderDistance 0 0 to 512 How far away, in blocks, the glass and flowers of a Faerie Vase are drawn. 0 draws them as far as the world is drawn (your render distance); a lower number saves drawing in builds with many vases.
portraitCacheSize 64 8 to 512 How many familiar portraits (the heads on satchel icons) are kept ready to draw.
portraitPaintsPerFrame 2 1 to 16 How many familiar portraits are painted in one frame, at most, when many are needed at once.

[advanced.shaders]¶

Shaderpack compatibility. Each list names shaderpacks by a word in their names, whatever its case ("solas" matches "Solas Shader v3.8.zip"), so a renamed or forked pack can be added. A change to a list redraws the world's blocks.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
forceParticleSnow false true / false Let the realm's snow fall as particles even without a shaderpack (it does under any shaderpack, which cannot draw it the other way). For a shaderpack the mod does not recognize as one.
softGlowPacks solas a list Shaderpacks that light glowing blocks too steeply (washing them out white): the mod's glowing faces are drawn a little dimmer under them.
faintGlowPacks bsl
complementary		 a list Shaderpacks that light a block's own glow too faintly: huge pastel mushroom caps are drawn brighter under them.
strongLanternGlowPacks solas
bliss
bsl
makeup		 a list Shaderpacks that make the Twiggy Faerie Lantern glare: it is drawn deeper under them.
eyesNoLightPacks photon
bliss		 a list Shaderpacks that cannot draw a soft glow through the game's eyes pass (as a spider's eyes): faeries' shine and a brewing cauldron's sigil are not drawn so under them.
eyesDoubledPacks bsl a list Shaderpacks that draw the eyes pass about twice as bright as the game: faeries' shine is given half under them.
eyesTextureOnlyPacks makeup a list Shaderpacks that draw the eyes pass from the texture's color alone: faeries' shine is drawn from a tinted texture, and fainter, under them.
