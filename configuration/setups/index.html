Ready-made setups¶

Copy a block into the file its title names (where are they?): a block with no title goes in faeriehollow-server.toml, the front page. Replace the lines with the same names, and save. Each setup changes only the lines shown; mix and match as you like.

Every example here is checked

These blocks are tested against the mod's real settings whenever these docs are built, so the names and values are always valid for the current version.

A cozy, peaceful realm¶

The realm's foes stop coming: no goblin packs, patrols or bombers, no Mandrakes, Puffcaps or Satyr Pipers, no feathered serpents, no night reapers. Whispurrs and the realm's gentle creatures still come.

[realm]
    hostileSpawns = false

Note

Satyr Pipers are foes: at their revels they turn on anyone who steps into the ring, so their doings stop too.

This doesn't cover the realm's folk who live in their own places (minotaurs in their ruins, kobolds in their mines), the Bedsheet Ghosts that drift about ruins by night, or bosses you summon yourself. To stop every creature the realm brings, set the doMobSpawning and doPatrolSpawning game rules to false. Peaceful difficulty stops the monsters only.

Gentler nights, not none¶

Foes still come, but half as often, and they're a little weaker.

[realm]
    spawnRatePercent = 50

[foes]
    healthPercent = 75
    damagePercent = 75

Or fine-tune each one in realm.toml:

faeriehollow-server/realm.toml
[spawns.goblinCamps]
    followersMax = 2
    refillTicks = 72000

[spawns.goblinPatrols]
    firstDay = 5
    goblinsMax = 3

[spawns.goblinBombers]
    chance = 10
    cap = 1

[spawns.reapers]
    chance = 10
    cap = 1

A harder realm¶

Foes hit harder and take more to bring down, and the Greater Giant most of all.

[foes]
    healthPercent = 150
    damagePercent = 125
    bossHealthPercent = 150

Quicker bonds¶

Familiars reach each bond level in about half the time.

[familiars]
    affectionPercent = 200

Or, for finer control, in familiars.toml: less affection needed per level, more for gathering, and familiars hungry for treats more often.

faeriehollow-server/familiars.toml
[bond]
    curveBase = 10.0
    itemsPerAffectionPoint = 16
    treatCooldownTicks = 3000

A long-term bond¶

Twice the levels, each taking longer: for servers that play for months. Every perk still runs from level 1 to the highest level, so each level gives a little less, and each familiar power unlocks as far along the bond as before.

[familiars]
    maxBondLevel = 20
    affectionPercent = 67

Tip

Lowering maxBondLevel later never takes affection away: a familiar past the new top level stays at it, and has all its affection back if you raise it again.

Stronger (or gentler) familiar powers¶

Every familiar power half as strong again: wider wards, longer breath, likelier finds, and waits between uses shortened to match.

[familiars]
    powerPercent = 150

Or only one power, or one kind of familiar, in familiars.toml:

faeriehollow-server/familiars.toml
[powers.creeperWard]
    powerPercent = 125

[familiars.wolf]
    powerPercent = 75

Satchels as plain backpacks¶

No gathering for anyone, and every satchel opens all 27 slots from the start.

[familiars]
    gathering = false
faeriehollow-server/familiars.toml
[satchel]
    slotsMin = 27
    slotsMax = 27

Quieter familiars¶

Familiars stop chatting in speech bubbles. Or, to keep the chatter but drop the nagging, keep the bubbles and have a hungry familiar mention it only once:

[familiars]
    speechBubbles = false
faeriehollow-server/familiars.toml
[speech]
    hungerReminderTicks = 0

Each player can also hide bubbles for themselves, or turn familiars' chatter down on the vanilla Voice/Speech slider: see Client settings.

No faerie lights¶

Light-giving familiars (faeries, the glow squid, Titania and Oberon, and any custom familiar with the illuminate ability) stop lighting the ground around them as you walk (handy for builders and redstone).

faeriehollow-server/familiars.toml
[companion]
    faerieLight = false

Plain trees¶

Witch's Willows and faerie trees grow with plain log branches and no roots, like vanilla trees. Trees already grown keep their shape.

faeriehollow-server/world.toml
[features]
    limbsAndRoots = false

No goblin camps or minotaur ruins¶

The realm's goblin camps and minotaur ruins stop generating in land generated from then on.

[world]
    disabledStructures = ["goblin_camp", "minotaur_ruins"]

Lighter on a busy server¶

Fewer creatures kept near each player, a smaller search for an existing portal on each trip (loading less of the world), and familiars gathering a little less often.

faeriehollow-server/familiars.toml
[gathering]
    fastestIntervalTicks = 10.0
faeriehollow-server/realm.toml
[portal]
    searchRadius = 32

[spawns.whispurrs]
    cap = 1

[spawns.brewFoes]
    puffcapCap = 4

Whispurrs everywhere they haunt¶

Wild Whispurrs come more often to their ruins, villages and lands, and trust you sooner.

faeriehollow-server/realm.toml
[spawns.whispurrs]
    ruinsChance = 50
    villageChance = 15
    landsChance = 25
faeriehollow-server/familiars.toml
[whispurr]
    trustPlays = 2
