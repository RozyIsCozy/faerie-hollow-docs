Familiar settings¶

Every setting in faeriehollow-server/familiars.toml, in the order it appears in the file. The front page (faeriehollow-server.toml) holds the bond's highest level, gathering's and speech's on/off, which creatures can't be bonded, and the percentages applied on top of these.

Powers and percentages

A familiar power's strength is its familiar's own value (its species data) times the power's powerPercent here, the familiar's own powerPercent here, and the front page's powerPercent. Only amounts scale (a ward's reach, a breath, a chance); waits between uses shorten to match, and the level a power unlocks at never changes. enabled = false switches a power off for every familiar that has it.

[bond]¶

How a familiar's affection grows into bond levels (the highest level is on the front page).

Setting Default Allowed What it does
curveBase 20.0 1.0 to 10000.0 Affection needed for level L, in all: round(curveBase * (L - 1) ^ curveExponent).
curveExponent 1.5 1.0 to 4.0 See the setting above.
companionshipIntervalTicks 1200 (1 min) 20 to 72000 Ticks a familiar must spend accompanying you to earn companionship affection (1200 = 1 minute).
companionshipPoints 1 0 to 1000 Affection earned each time.
itemsPerAffectionPoint 32 1 to 100000 Items a familiar must gather to earn one affection point.
treatPoints 6 0 to 1000 Affection gained when you feed your familiar one of its treats.
treatCooldownTicks 6000 (5 min) 0 to 1728000 Ticks before a familiar is hungry for another treat (6000 = 5 minutes).
feyFoodPercent 50 0 to 100 Affection from Fey Food, which any familiar eats, as a percentage of treatPoints. It shares the treat cooldown.
cordialPercent 400 0 to 10000 Affection from Kindred Cordial, brewed in a Bewitched Cauldron, as a percentage of treatPoints. Any familiar takes it from its owner. It shares the treat cooldown.
favorPercent 150 100 to 1000 How fast a familiar's affection grows while its owner carries Faerie Favor, as a percentage of its usual pace (its species' pace and all, Titania's and Oberon's among them). 100 makes the amulet do nothing.
unlockLevelsFollowMaxLevel true true / false The bond levels powers open at (Titania's and Oberon's gifts, an axolotl's mending...) are written for a highest level of 10. true: on a server whose highest level is another, each is moved to match (with 20, a gift of level 10 opens at 20, one of level 3 at 5). false: each stays at its own level, and one past the highest opens at the highest.

[gathering]¶

Item gathering (switched on or off for everyone on the front page). Each value is reached at level 1 (min, slowest) and at the highest level (max, fastest).

Setting Default Allowed What it does
radiusMin 3.0 0.5 to 64.0 Reach in blocks.
radiusMax 12.0 0.5 to 64.0 See the setting above.
slowestIntervalTicks 30.0 (1.5 s) 1.0 to 1200.0 Ticks between gathering rounds.
fastestIntervalTicks 5.0 (0.25 s) 1.0 to 1200.0 See the setting above.
batchMin 1 1 to 64 Items handled at once.
batchMax 4 1 to 64 See the setting above.
ignoreOwnerDrops true true / false Leave items the owner threw away on purpose.
forageCooldownTicks 600 (30 s) 0 to 72000 Ticks a foraging familiar (a fox, chicken, frog...) waits after a roll for a find before it may roll again (600 = 30 seconds), whatever it gathers, so a stream of items from a dropper finds no more than ordinary gathering does; one wait shared by all its forager powers. 0 rolls for every gathered stack.

[gathering.chase]¶

How a familiar runs after loot itself.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
chaseFromOwner 11.0 0.0 to 11.0 How far from its owner (blocks) a familiar runs after loot itself; farther, it only draws it in. 0: it never runs after loot.
chaseSpeed 1.6 0.1 to 4.0 How fast a walking familiar runs after loot, as a multiple of its walking speed.
flierChaseSpeed 0.35 0.05 to 2.0 How fast a flying familiar goes after loot, in blocks a tick.
chaseTimeoutTicks 100 (5 s) 1 to 1200 Ticks it runs after one find before it gives up and draws it in instead.

[gathering.pull]¶

How a familiar draws loot in.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
pullSpeedMin 0.25 0.01 to 4.0 How fast loot it draws in flies to it, in blocks a tick, at level 1 and at the highest level.
pullSpeedMax 0.8 0.01 to 4.0 See the setting above.
maxReach 64.0 1.0 to 128.0 The farthest any familiar reaches for loot (blocks), whatever its reach and its kind's own multiplier say: a bound that keeps its searches cheap.

[satchel]¶

The Familiar Satchel's pockets. A bag has 27 slots; slotsMin of them are open without a familiar or at level 1, growing evenly to slotsMax at the highest level. Items in a slot that closes stay there and can still be taken out.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
slotsMin 9 0 to 27 Open pockets without a familiar or at level 1, and at the highest level.
slotsMax 27 0 to 27 See the setting above.

[companion]¶

A familiar out in the world beside you.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
teleportDistance 16.0 12.0 to 128.0 A familiar farther than this (blocks) from its owner teleports back to them. Never below 12, the furthest a familiar races its owner to loot.
faintRecoveryTicks 1200 (1 min) 20 to 72000 Ticks a familiar that fights (a wolf) rests in its Familiar Satchel after it faints, before it comes back out (1200 = 1 minute).
fightMemoryTicks 100 (5 s) 1 to 1200 How long a familiar that fights (a wolf) stays in its owner's fight after the last blow traded, in ticks; also how long it remembers a foe.
mealsHeal true true / false Feeding a hurt familiar heals it (by the food's nourishment), even when it is not hungry.
faerieLight true true / false Light-giving familiars (faeries, Titania and Oberon, the glow squid) light up the blocks round them as they go, like a carried lantern: the light follows them and is gone behind them. false: they still glow, but light up nothing round them.

[companion.following]¶

How a familiar keeps up with its owner.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
followStartDistance 6.0 1.0 to 12.0 Farther than this from its owner (blocks), a familiar walks after them; also how near it must be to climb up behind them on a unicorn.
stuckTeleportTicks 40 (2 s) 1 to 1200 Ticks a familiar may be stuck inside a block before it is moved beside its owner.
respawnDelayTicks 40 (2 s) 1 to 1200 Ticks before a familiar killed or unloaded comes back out of its satchel (five times as long after it could not be brought out).

[speech.health]¶

When familiars speak of hurts, as fractions of health (0 to 1).

Setting Default Allowed What it does
hurtBelow 0.3333333333333333 0.0 to 1.0 A familiar that fights says it is badly hurt at or below this share of its health...
healedAbove 0.5 0.0 to 1.0 ...and says so again only once it has healed past this share.
ownerHurtBelow 0.3 0.0 to 1.0 It says you are badly hurt at or below this share of your health...
ownerHealedAbove 0.6 0.0 to 1.0 ...and again only once you have healed past this share.

[powers]¶

Each familiar power: whether it works, how strong it is (a percentage, on top of the front page's powerPercent and each familiar's own), and its own timings. Each familiar's exact numbers (a cat's ward radius...) are its species data, which datapacks can change.

[powers.illuminate]¶

Illuminate: a faerie's, Titania's and Oberon's light, and the glow squid's. (companion.faerieLight switches off only the light they place in the world.)

Setting Default Allowed What it does
enabled true true / false Whether this power works. false: a familiar that has it already out puts it away at once (undoing anything it placed), and takes it up again as soon as it is switched back on.
powerPercent 100 0 to 1000 How strong this power is, as a percentage (on top of each familiar's own and the front page's powerPercent): its reach, its chance or its count grow with it, the waits between its uses shorten to match, and the bond level it opens at never changes. 0 leaves it at its weakest; enabled = false switches it off.
