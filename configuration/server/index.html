Server settings: the front page¶

faeriehollow-server.toml is the place to start: the settings most server owners look for, and the percentages that scale whole groups of finer settings at once (100 changes nothing). To find it, see Where the files are. Changes apply as soon as you save it.

The fine tuning is in the faeriehollow-server folder beside it, one file a subject:

File Covers
familiars.toml The bond and its levels, gathering, the satchel, speech, every familiar power and every kind of familiar
creatures.toml Each foe and boss, and the realm's gentle creatures, mounts and merchants
realm.toml The Faerie Portal, everything the realm brings near its players, its doings, tea parties, mushrooms, plants and snow
world.toml Which of the realm's structures and features generate, and the mod's loot in vanilla's chests
items.toml The Bewitched Cauldron and its brews, the Faerie Altar, the wedding cake, gear, food and trees

Switching something off

hostileSpawns = false (in [realm] below) stops every foe the realm brings near its players. Each one also has its own enabled in realm.toml. See Ready-made setups for complete examples.

[familiars]¶

Familiars: the creatures players bond with and carry in a Familiar Satchel (familiars.toml holds the rest).

Setting Default Allowed What it does
gathering true true / false Familiars gather loose items for their owners. Each player can pause their own familiar's gathering with a key (G by default); false switches gathering off for everyone, whatever they choose.
speechBubbles true true / false Familiars say what is on their mind in speech bubbles, in their own voice: when they are hungry, when their Familiar Satchel is full, when you are hurt, when they are fed or petted, and more. Each player can also hide them for themselves in their own settings.
maxBondLevel 10 1 to 100 The highest bond level a familiar reaches. Lowering it never takes affection away: a familiar past it stays at the new top level and has every point back if it is raised again.
affectionPercent 100 0 to 1000 How fast every familiar's bond grows, as a percentage of its usual pace (200 is twice as fast, 50 half).
powerPercent 100 0 to 1000 How strong every familiar's powers are, as a percentage (150 half as strong again: a wider ward, a longer breath, a likelier find, and waits between uses shortened to match). Each power and each kind of familiar has its own in familiars.toml, applied on top of this.
disabledFamiliars [] a list Creatures that may not be bonded as familiars on this server, each its id, e.g. "minecraft:wolf" or "faeriehollow:titania". One already bonded stays in its satchel and can still be released.

[realm]¶

The Faerie Realm (realm.toml holds the rest).

Setting Default Allowed What it does
portals true true / false The Faerie Portal opens and takes travelers to the realm and back. false keeps every portal closed: no one can reach the realm (players already there can still leave through a portal home).
hostileSpawns true true / false Foes come near players in the Faerie Realm: goblin packs, patrols and bombers, night reapers, feathered serpents, Mandrakes and Puffcaps, and Satyr Pipers. false stops every one of them, whatever the realm file sets.
spawnRatePercent 100 0 to 1000 Scales the chance of everything the realm brings near its players (its foes, Whispurrs, ghosts and satyrs' doings), as a percentage of each spawner's own chance (realm.toml). 0 brings nothing.
mushroomWard true true / false Pastel mushrooms (glowcaps), growing or potted, keep monsters from spawning near them, in every dimension (realm.toml sets how near, and in which dimensions). false: they ward nothing.

[foes]¶

The mod's foes and bosses: goblins, kobolds, minotaurs, brew foes, serpents, night reapers, trolls and giants. Each percentage scales every foe, on top of each foe's own in creatures.toml.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
healthPercent 100 1 to 1000 Every foe's health, as a percentage of its own, on top of each foe's own healthPercent. The game holds every creature to 1024 health at most, however high the percentages go.
damagePercent 100 0 to 1000 The damage every foe deals, every kind of blow (bites, slings, bolts, tosses), as a percentage of its own.
dropsPercent 100 0 to 1000 How much every foe drops when slain, as a percentage (0 drops nothing; past 100, each stack grows to match).
experiencePercent 100 0 to 1000 The experience every foe gives when slain, as a percentage.
bossHealthPercent 100 1 to 1000 The bosses' (the Greater Giant's) health, as a percentage, on top of healthPercent. The game holds every creature to 1024 health at most, however high the percentages go.
bossDamagePercent 100 0 to 1000 The damage the bosses deal, as a percentage, on top of damagePercent.
greaterGiantCanWake true true / false The Greater Giant can be woken at Giant's Wake with the Horn of the Old War. false: he sleeps on.
bombersBreakBlocks true true / false Goblin bombers' blasts break blocks (only where the mobGriefing gamerule allows it). false: they hurt, but break nothing.

[world]¶

World generation and loot (world.toml holds the rest). World generation changes apply to land generated from then on.

Setting Default Allowed What it does
disabledStructures [] a list Realm structures that no longer generate, each its name as in world.toml's structures section, e.g. "minotaur_ruins" or "goblin_camp". Mind that Giant's Wake ("giants_wake") is where the Greater Giant sleeps and the first source of a Giant's Tooth.
crownPiecesInVanillaLoot true true / false Broken Crown Pieces turn up in vanilla's chests and fishing as well as the realm's relic chests.

[items]¶

Items and blocks (items.toml holds the rest).

Setting Default Allowed What it does
weddingCakeFlight true true / false A slice of Faerie Wedding Cake gives faerie wings that fly. false: a slice only slows your fall.
pixieDraughtShrinks true true / false The Pixie Draught shrinks whoever drinks it.
brewTimePercent 100 1 to 1000 How long the Bewitched Cauldron takes to brew, as a percentage of each brew's own time.
