Speech bubbles and translations¶

All of Faerie Hollow's text (names, tooltips, the Grimoire's pages and every speech bubble) is in language files, assets/faeriehollow/lang/<language>.json. A resource pack can change any line, or add a language.

Changing a few lines¶

Your pack only needs the lines you change. Anything you leave out keeps the mod's text.

assets/faeriehollow/lang/en_us.json
{
  "item.faeriehollow.familiar_satchel": "Familiar Backpack",
  "speech.minecraft.cat.hungry.1": "Feed me, mortal."
}

Speech bubble lines¶

Familiars' lines are numbered keys:

speech.<namespace>.<mob>.<remark>.<number>

For example speech.minecraft.cat.hungry.1, speech.minecraft.cat.hungry.2... Each time, a familiar picks one of the numbered lines at random. Numbers start at 1 and are counted until the first one missing, so leave no gaps: after .1, .2, .3, add .4 (a .5 after a missing .4 is never said). Up to 64 lines per remark.

Remark When it's said
hungry It could eat a treat again.
bag_full Its satchel's open slots are all in use.
fed It was fed a treat or Fey Food.
petted Its owner petted it.
level_up Its bond grew a level.
bonded It has just become its owner's familiar.
sleepy Its owner lay down to sleep.
worried Its owner is badly hurt.
debuffed Fighters only: something harmful was put on it (poison, weakness...).
hurt Fighters only: it is badly hurt.
recovered Fighters only: it is back after fainting.
dancing Faeries: given their favorite treat.
gathered Faeries: picked something up (once in a long while).
crown, queen, king Faeries: shown a piece of a fallen monarch's crown, or a shard of the Moon or Sun.
royal_crown, own_shard, mourn_queen, mourn_king Titania and Oberon: shown their own relics, or their consort's.

A remark a familiar has no lines for is said with the shared lines in speech.faeriehollow.familiar.<remark>.<number>.

Lines for a modded creature

A custom familiar says its own mob's lines, so a familiar made from othermod:otter reads speech.othermod.otter.hungry.1 and so on. Add them in a resource pack's language file, or point its voice.lines at another mob's lines.

Translating¶

Faerie Hollow is already translated into 13 languages. To add another, or improve one:

  1. Copy the English file, assets/faeriehollow/lang/en_us.json (from the mod's jar), to your language's code, for example nl_nl.json, in a resource pack.
  2. Translate the text on the right of each line. Leave the keys on the left as they are.
  3. Keep every %s: the game fills them in with names and numbers, in order. If your sentence needs them in another order, number them: %2$s ... %1$s.
  4. Use your language's own Minecraft words (for "spawn egg", "sneak", "inventory"...) so it reads like the rest of the game.
