Compatibility¶

Mod Status Notes
Curios Supported, optional Adds a satchel slot and a necklace slot.
Iris / Oculus with shaderpacks Supported, optional Tuned for popular packs.
Sodium / Embeddium Works Nothing to set up.
Dynamic Trees Works alongside Faerie Hollow's trees stay its own.
Recipe viewers (JEI, EMI, REI) No special support yet Normal crafting recipes show as usual.

Curios¶

Faerie Hollow never needs Curios. With it installed, players get:

  • a Familiar Satchel slot in Curios' menu. Each player chooses where to wear their satchel: the built-in slot or the Curios one, one satchel at a time. It works the same in either.
  • a necklace slot (Curios' own kind) for Faerie Favor, which works there just as when carried.

In the Curios slot, Curios' own rules apply: its render toggle hides the satchel on your back, and its keepCurios setting decides whether the satchel drops when you die.

Supported versions: Curios 5.3.0 or newer on 1.20.1, 9.2.0 or newer on 1.21.1.

For modpack makers¶

Let the satchel into other Curios slots

Add faeriehollow:familiar_satchel to that slot's item tag, for example data/curios/tags/item/back.json (1.21.1) or data/curios/tags/items/back.json (1.20.1):

{ "values": ["faeriehollow:familiar_satchel"] }
Remove Faerie Hollow's Curios slots

Override its slot assignments in a datapack, giving them to nobody:

data/faeriehollow/curios/entities/familiar_satchel.json
{ "entities": [], "slots": ["familiar_satchel"] }
data/faeriehollow/curios/entities/faerie_favour.json
{ "entities": [], "slots": ["necklace"] }

Removing Curios from a world

Take every satchel (and every other curio) out of the Curios slots first. Moving a world from 1.20.1 to 1.21.1 also loses Curios slots: see Updating and moving worlds.

Shaderpacks¶

With a shader loader (Iris on 1.21.1, Oculus on 1.20.1), Faerie Hollow's plants and leaves sway and its lights glow like their vanilla cousins, and the Faerie Realm's sky keeps its pink and purple colors. Glow and light are tuned for:

  • Complementary
  • BSL
  • Bliss
  • Photon
  • MakeUp - Ultra Fast
  • Solas

Other packs work too; these are the ones checked by hand. Without a shader loader, everything draws normally.

Sodium and Embeddium¶

Faerie Hollow works with Sodium (1.21.1) and Embeddium (1.20.1) with nothing to set up, including the realm's colored water.

Dynamic Trees¶

Faerie Hollow's trees are its own whether or not Dynamic Trees is installed: Witch's Willow and faerie saplings grow the mod's own trees, felled and broken as usual. Dynamic Trees only takes over saplings it has a species for, and it has none of Faerie Hollow's.

Recipe viewers¶

There's no special support for JEI, EMI or REI yet. Ordinary crafting, smelting and stonecutting recipes show in them as usual, but Bewitched Cauldron brews don't. In game, the Witch's Grimoire lists every brew the world knows, including those added by datapacks.

Other mods¶

  • Modded mobs can be familiars with a datapack: see Custom familiars.
  • Modded seeds work in Fey Food through the loaders' common seeds tag.
  • Modded biomes can host Faerie Hollow's creatures and structures through tags.
