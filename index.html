Faerie Hollow Docs¶

Welcome! This is the place to set Faerie Hollow up just the way you like it: every setting, every datapack hook, every sound you can swap, and answers to the questions people ask most.

Looking for something specific?

Press / or S to search every page. Searching a setting's name (like speechBubbles) or a tag (like treats/cat) takes you straight to it.
