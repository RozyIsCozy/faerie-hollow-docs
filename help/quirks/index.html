Known quirks and edge cases¶

Things that work as intended but can surprise people.

Familiars and satchels¶

  • Familiars never go through portals themselves. When you change dimension, your familiar is packed into its satchel and comes back out beside you on the other side.
  • Only /kill or the void can kill a familiar that doesn't fight. It then drops nothing and comes back out of its satchel two seconds later. A fighter faints even then (below).
  • A fighter faints instead of dying. It rests in its satchel for a minute (companion.faintRecoveryTicks), and the satchel's tooltip counts down.
  • Closed satchel slots keep their items. If slots close (a familiar is released, or the server lowers satchel.slotsMax in familiars.toml), what's in them stays and can still be taken out, but nothing new goes in.
  • Lowering the highest bond level never takes affection away. A familiar past the new top stays at it, and gets every point back if the level is raised again.
  • Items from dispensers and droppers are gathered, but earn no affection. On 1.21.1, so are items from crafters, vaults and trial spawners.
  • A satchel can't be in two slots at once. With Curios, a player wears one satchel, in either the built-in slot or the Curios slot.

The Faerie Portal¶

  • Players stand in the portal for a moment before being taken (portal.playerDelayTicks in realm.toml); creative players and anything else that steps in go at once.
  • If there's no portal near where you arrive, one is grown for you: a Witch's Willow with its hollow already open, on the most level open ground nearby, never in place of anything built.
  • To go back through, step out first. Having come through, you can't use a portal again until you've stepped out of it for a moment.
  • Open screens close as you go, as with a nether portal.
  • The wither and the ender dragon never go through.
  • Breaking any block of the portal's rim closes it. The trunk is given back as it was.

Version differences¶

1.20.1 (Forge) 1.21.1 (NeoForge)
Server settings files One set per world, in serverconfig/ One set for the game, in config/ (a world may have its own copy)
Datapack folders Plural (tags/items, recipes) Singular (tags/item, recipe)
Brew recipe result "item": ... "id": ...
Datapack painting variants Not possible Possible
Gathering from crafters, vaults, trial spawners (not in 1.20.1) Yes

Multiplayer¶

  • The server's settings win. Players can't change familiar, gathering or realm settings for themselves.
  • Other players can milk your cow, mooshroom or goat familiar, but nothing else.
  • Sleeping counts across the overworld and the realm together, by playersSleepingPercentage.
