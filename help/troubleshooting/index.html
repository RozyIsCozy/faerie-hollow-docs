Troubleshooting¶

The game won't start, or says Faerie Hollow is missing something¶

  • Check you have the right jar for your loader: the 1.20.1 jar is for Forge, the 1.21.1 jar for NeoForge.
  • Check your loader is new enough: see What you need.
  • If you use Curios, check it's at least the version listed there. An older Curios stops the game loading.

I can't join a server¶

Both sides need Faerie Hollow, in the same version. Compare the version in your mods list with the server's. If the server uses Curios, you need it too.

My setting changes did nothing¶

  1. Is it the right file? Each setting belongs to one file: the front page or one of the topic files in the faeriehollow-server folder. On Forge 1.20.1 each world has its own server settings, inside the world's serverconfig/ folder. On NeoForge 1.21.1, check whether the world has its own copy in serverconfig/: that copy wins over the one in config/. See Where the files are.
  2. Is the setting in its [section]? A setting outside its section is reset. Compare with the settings reference.
  3. Is the value in range? Out-of-range numbers are pulled back to the nearest allowed value.
  4. Does it only affect new things? Some settings (tea parties, trees) only change what's made from then on.
  5. Look in the game log (logs/latest.log) for lines mentioning faeriehollow: the loader reports any value it had to correct.

A datapack change did nothing¶

  1. Run /datapack list and check your pack is listed and enabled.
  2. Check the folder names for your version (tags/item or tags/items...): see The two versions use different folder names.
  3. Check whether the change needs a restart or new land instead of /reload: see When changes apply.
  4. Check the game log for your file's name. A custom familiar with any mistake is skipped whole, and the log says what was wrong.
  5. Check your JSON is valid (a missing comma or quote stops the whole file loading). An online JSON checker helps.

My familiar won't bond¶

  • You must be wearing an empty Familiar Satchel, and sneak + right-click the creature with an empty hand.
  • The creature must be won over: tamed by you (cats, wolves, parrots...), trusting you (foxes, ocelots), released from a bucket (axolotls), and so on.
  • It can't be on someone else's lead, tied to a fence, or ridden by another player. Let it off first.

My familiar stopped picking things up¶

  • Did you press G? It pauses gathering; press it again. The satchel screen's Gathering line says (paused).
  • Is the satchel full? Only its open slots take items; then your inventory.
  • Has the server switched gathering off (gathering.enabled = false)? G then says so.
  • Is the item in #faeriehollow:familiars_ignore, or one you threw away yourself?

The realm looks wrong with shaders¶

  • Update your shaderpack and shader loader.
  • Try one of the packs Faerie Hollow is tuned for.

Seasonal music doesn't play¶

  • The Faerie Hollow Seasonal Music resource pack must be enabled in Options > Resource Packs.
  • Seasonal Realm Music must be on in the options screen.
  • It plays only in its seasons (25 to 31 October, 21 to 31 December), by your computer's date.

Still stuck?¶

Note your Minecraft version, loader version, Faerie Hollow version and other mods, and include your logs/latest.log when you ask for help.
